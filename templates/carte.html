    Method. A venue is any permanent organisation with a building of its own, backed by a public authority (state, Land, canton, region or municipality depending on the system) and charged with presenting or creating work. Funded companies without a building, and festivals, are excluded, which mechanically understates Italy, Spain and the Netherlands. A school is a specialised, degree-granting higher education course backed by a state institution, counted by establishment rather than by programme: an academy carrying four puppetry degrees counts once. Russia and Turkey are counted for their European parts only, which is why the puppetry course at Eskisehir does not appear in the Turkish figure. Andorra, Monaco, San Marino, the Vatican and Liechtenstein sit at zero in the data. The ranges shown carry real uncertainty: the map colours the midpoint. Every school marker can be hovered for its address, award and link.
